# Jev anti-jobs

Keep Jev out of math, writing, and irreversible execution.

| Job | Who |
|-----|-----|
| Compute / determinism | Code |
| Create prose, plans, drafts | LLM |
| Decide next move at forks | Jev |
| Irreversible side effects | Human Approve or pre-agreed policy |

Illegal: Jev drafting the email. Jev issuing the refund. Jev merging the PR. Using Jev as a calculator.
