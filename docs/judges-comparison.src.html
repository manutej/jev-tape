Method

What each judge saw, and what the clock timed

One settled page, one claim, three judges. The page was loaded once by Vibium and left alone; the judges differ only in what they were handed and how they answer. The catalog holds eight public pages with one claim each, six true and two false by construction.

The protocol, step by step

  1. Vibium opens the page headless and waits for load. Nothing is clicked. The same daemon session serves every judge on that page.
  2. Jev gets one typed request: the page text excerpt, the element-map labels, the url and title, and the claim as a question with three answers. The runner times the fetch round trip. Answers below the peakedness floor escalate instead of deciding.
  3. Vibium's check gets the claim and the live browser. It runs its own tool loop with the model until it answers or its budget ends. The runner times the process.
  4. A screenshot of the same page is saved, copied under a neutral name in a directory holding only images, and handed to a fresh Sonnet agent with the claim and nothing else. The agent stamps the clock before opening the image and after deciding. Its lifetime from spawn to hand-back is kept as a second, slower number.
  5. Every verdict is scored against the catalog label. A judge that declines to decide is counted as an escalate, never as right or wrong.
  6. The whole thing is run again on another day. Verdict agreement between runs and the timing spread are reported per judge.

What the proxy is not. Claude computer use was not available in the session, so the screenshot judge stands in for the vision half of it: one frame, no desktop, no planner. Its numbers say how fast a vision model reads and judges a page, not how the computer-use product performs end to end.

Whole task

The same login, three ways, stage by stage

A known flow on the local fixture site: open, fill two fields, submit, confirm the secure area. Jev gates the submit and verifies the landing; the pixel operator sees only screenshots and clicks coordinates; the model plans, acts and verifies with Vibium's own loop.

Reading the numbers

    Does not

    • ⊘ Accuracy out of sample. The claims were written with the pages known and thresholds are hand-set. The 140-row catalog with blind claims is the measurement that strikes this.
    • ⊘ Anything about Claude computer use as a product, or about Sonnet driving a browser at scale; one login by pixel operator is a single sample.
    • ⊘ Model-loop time on a fast day. The check times include the model's own variance and one 142 s outlier on the Cloudflare-walled page; the second run shows how much of that repeats.
