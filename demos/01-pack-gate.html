The legend was two lines short.

In the depth experiment, arm B fell to 0.25 at depth 7 while arm A held 0.71. The pack was lossless for its builder and unreadable for its reader: nobody had written down what - meant. Two legend lines later, the arms matched exactly. This demo puts that check where it belongs, before the model reads the pack, as one batched judge call recorded on the tape.

What the tape shows

The re-expansion check is deterministic, so it is code: no judge, no record. If it fails, the run is a local RED and the judge is never asked. A Choice from the judge cannot override a local RED.

When re-expansion passes, one request carries every question about the legend. The answer map comes back once and is written to Event History once. Replay reuses it. The pack ships only after the gate is GREEN, which is the apply-last rule with a pack in the place of a domain command.

Flip the two legend lines and watch the right-hand chart: it switches between the raw run and the rerun after the fix. Those are numbers measured in the experiment, not the judge's opinion. The judge, recorded or twinned, only decides whether the legend is complete; the chart shows what an incomplete legend cost.

In the default state the recorded judge agrees with the twin on both nouls and then chooses GREEN for reader_spec_equivalent at only P=0.39, where the twin would have chosen RED. Code does not take the label at its word: the θ line and the top_prob_floor both fail, so the Choice is downgraded and the sentinel noul, which fails its gate, makes the verdict RED. That disagreement is a finding, and the reasons list prints it.

Counterpoint

A GREEN gate is not comprehension. The gate checks that the legend defines what the pack uses. It says nothing about whether a weak model can enumerate a twenty-name list at depth 7, where both arms fell equally. Consistency of spec is necessary, not sufficient.
