demo 02 of 10 · apply-last square

A refactor is a square, and the square is the gate.

In the witness, loans.LoansService#log was renamed to #writeAudit and the three public ports stayed fixed. The swap of accounts and savings commuted with that refactor. Draw it as a square: the top edge is the old unit glued to its repository, the bottom edge is the new unit glued the same way. If the two ways round the square agree, the merge is safe. This demo runs that square as the apply-last loop: two judged gates, one POST each, a crash while parked, a replay that costs nothing, and a human at the merge.

The intersection. wiring-and-the-whole checks a migration square: parts glued the same way before and after must give the same whole. jev-tape asks typed questions before a write. A merge is a write. The square is the question.

What the tape shows

Seven steps, and only two of them talk to the judge. assertLegalCommand is code: RefactorUnit is a legal command, and MergeToDefault is on the list of commands a human must release. propose is code too: the refactored unit is glued to its repository by the same pushout that glued the original, which is a gluing along shared names with no judgement in it.

Each gate is one request. The task gate carries three questions about the rename map; the output gate carries three about the square. Each answer map is written to the tape once. When the worker crashes while parked, the tape keeps both. Replay relights the ring from history and makes zero new POSTs. The merge itself waits for a person, because a merge to the default branch is a write that cannot happen twice.

Flip the toggle to rename a port as well. The ports question falls to 0.10 and the task gate goes RED before the square is ever drawn. The judge's Choice still leans GREEN, and it does not matter: a Choice cannot override a failed gate.

Counterpoint

A commuting square is not a passing test. It says the refactored part glues to its neighbours the way the old part did, at the level of names and signatures. It says nothing about behaviour. The witness checked a rename on a 16-file toy; the same square on a real migration is the shape of the check, not its proof.
The judge here is a twin. The nouls are set from the witness booleans and the toggle, deterministically, with the same wire shape and contract checks as the real client. No key, no network. A live TypeSafe call is a claim this page does not make.
