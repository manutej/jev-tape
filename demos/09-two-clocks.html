One engine, two clocks. The block survives the crash.

Fifteen lines of an untrusted pack: twelve real ones from the E3 factored pack and three planted by this demo. The harness clock screens them in seconds, all fifteen in one request, and two are blocked. Then the process clock takes over: qualify, propose, qualify, apply, one step an hour. Crash the worker between the two and replay. The blocks come back from the tape, not from the judge.

The intersection. A wiring pack is text a model will read, and text a model will read is untrusted until screened. jev-tape's lane 3 screens it; its lane 1 gates the write that ships it. The same tape records both, on two clocks.

What the tape shows

Two clocks, one engine. The harness clock runs in seconds: fifteen lines, one request, one answer map, one cell on the tape with a POST mark. Its goal is fewer LLM calls, so the fifteen questions travel together. The process clock runs in hours: qualify the task, propose in memory, qualify the output, apply last. Its goal is the write that cannot happen twice.

Crash between them and replay. The screen's fifteen answers are already in Event History, so replay re-emits them without a POST, and the two blocked lines land as blocked again with a from tape tag. Then the loop continues from where it stopped: gates that never completed run now and are recorded now. The POST counter tells the story: 1 after the screen, 3 after both gates, and replay adds nothing.

The toggle shows the shape the spec calls unrepresentable. If the judge sat in Workflow code, replay would ask again, and a recorded BLOCK could come back as something else and be ingested. The banner is the demo's way of drawing a line that must not exist.

Counterpoint

The screen is a filter, not a security boundary. It routes lines before they reach the generator's context; it does not sandbox anything. A block on a line is not a judgement of the pack's author. The three planted lines are planted by this demo; they are not in the repo.
The judge here is a twin. Its rules are written in this page: a comment that contradicts the legend blocks, a mail verb or an entity outside the pack blocks, a duplicate delta skips. Same wire shape and contract checks as the real client, no key, no network. A live TypeSafe call is a claim this page does not make.
