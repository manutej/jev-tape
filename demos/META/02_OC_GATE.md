## The checks

- **topbar** — topbar mounted via JEV.topbar
- **hero** — hero with one-sentence h1 and the intersection aside
- **instrument** — instrument with controls and an SVG stage built in script
- **run** — primary button #run and a rewind action
- **tape** — Tape mounted and records at least one cell
- **twin** — judge is the twin (systemOne through JEV.twin) or page is path-0 only
- **compose-or-lane** — compose() used, or a lane verdict (pass/review/block/skip or verified/contradicted/unsupported)
- **tape-shows** — 'What the tape shows' section present
- **counterpoint** — two counterpoints, the second discloses the twin
- **provenance** — provenance table with ≥3 rows via JEV.provenanceTable
- **data-only** — loads only shared data/*.js and assets/tape.js; no external scripts
- **no-fetch** — never fetches, never names the key or the endpoint
- **no-libs** — no chart or icon libraries, no <img>
- **pin** — pin jev-1.13.0 appears (via topbar pill) and 'jev-latest' never does
- **no-caps-labels** — no ALL-CAPS label tells (text-transform:uppercase)
- **no-hype** — no hype words
- **viewport** — one viewport meta
- **reduced-motion** — respects prefers-reduced-motion in page script
- **untraced-numbers** — every number of two or more digits in the hero lede appears in `data/all.json` (structural constants such as 13, 16, 30, 40, 112, 514 are exempt).

## Collapse test (tier 1)

Total collapse: "is this a jev demo built to the plan?" answered by reading the page as a whole (the eye gate).
Full decomposition: the 19 checks above answered one at a time. The gate passes when both agree.
Where they disagreed during the build (a page that looked right but loaded an extra script, or passed every
check with an empty stage), the disagreement localised the defect to one edge, and that edge was fixed.
